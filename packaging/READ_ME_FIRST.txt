Roblox Analytics Agent (Windows)

1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\RobloxAnalyticsAgent.
   Keep both .exe files together.
2. Double-click RobloxAnalyticsAgent.exe. The setup screens walk you through the rest.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed yet
(signing costs money). Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

RobloxAnalyticsAgent.exe  the app (setup, daily report, inbox, settings)
roblox-agent.exe          the helper your AI agent starts; you don't need to open it

Everything stays on this computer. Source code and help:
https://github.com/Boking234/Rolytics
