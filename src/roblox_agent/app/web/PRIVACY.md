# Privacy Policy (draft)

Version 2026-11-13. Plain-language draft, not legal advice; to be reviewed before a public launch.

## The short version
Everything stays on your computer unless you switch on a feature that says otherwise. There are no accounts, no email, no passwords, no ads and no tracking.

## What stays on your computer
- Your Roblox API key (in a private file only your user can read).
- Your game's analytics, check-ups, inbox and settings (in a folder in your user profile).
- A random install number the app makes for itself. It isn't linked to your name.

## What goes where, and when
| Where | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Roblox | Your key, to read your game's numbers | Every check-up |
| Roblox's public game pages | Your game's ID, to show its name and icon | Once, during setup |
| Your AI agent | A short check-up text (a few hundred words), never the key | Only when you ask the agent |
| Discord (coming soon) | The short report text, sent through our relay | Only if you connect Discord |
| Community lessons (coming later) | Anonymized lessons only, see below | Only if you turn sharing on |

## If you turn on cloud sharing
Would be shared: the kind of problem (for example "crash rate up on phones"), how big it was as a percentage, your game's genre and size band, and whether a fix worked.
Never shared: your key, your game's name or ID, player names or IDs, exact revenue, your code, or anything about you.
Sharing is off by default, can't be turned on under 13, and can be turned off any time. Nothing uploads until the community feature launches; the app will tell you when it does.

## Players
The app only reads totals and averages Roblox already provides. It never sees individual players.

## Deleting everything
Uninstall the app and delete the data folder shown in Settings. Delete your key on Roblox's API Keys page.
