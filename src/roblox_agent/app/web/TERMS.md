# Terms of Service (draft)

Version 2026-11-13. This is a plain-language draft for testing with friends. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed before a public launch.

## What this app is
Roblox Analytics Agent is a free, open-source app (MIT license) that reads your Roblox game's analytics with an API key you create, stores them on your computer, and tells you what changed.

## Who can use it
- You must be allowed to use Roblox and Roblox's Creator Hub.
- If you are 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must read these terms with you and agree to them.
- If you are under 13, the app runs in local-only mode: Discord messages and cloud sharing are switched off.

## Your key and your game
- Only use a key for a game you own or are allowed to manage.
- The key stays on your computer. The app never shows it again, never sends it to an AI agent, and never uploads it.
- You can delete it any time (Settings, then Remove key) and should also delete it on Roblox's API Keys page.

## Advice, not guarantees
The check-ups are statistics and suggestions. They can be wrong. You decide what to change in your game. The app never changes your game by itself.

## Optional features
- **AI agents** (Claude, Codex and others) run on your own account and follow their own terms.
- **Discord messages** (coming soon) follow Discord's terms. You can stop them any time.
- **Cloud sharing** is off unless you turn it on. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what would be shared.
